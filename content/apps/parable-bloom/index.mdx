## Overview

A journey of mindfulness, puzzles, and faith. Guide vines through beautiful gardens and uncover timeless parables in this zen puzzler.

Parable Bloom is a cross-platform application built with Flutter, designed to offer a peaceful, meditative experience that combines logic puzzles with spiritual reflection.

[Privacy Policy](https://parable-bloom.pages.dev/privacy)

## Play Online & Download

Immerse yourself in the tranquility of Parable Bloom on your preferred platform:

- **Web**: Play instantly in your browser at [parable-bloom.web.app](https://parable-bloom.web.app/)
- **Android**: Get on [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.eng618.parablebloom) _(Currently in testing)_
- **iOS**: Coming soon to the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/parable-bloom/id1234567890)

## Features

- **Intuitive Gameplay**: Simple tap & swipe controls to guide growing vines through garden grid puzzles.
- **Beautiful Aesthetics**: Elegant visual styles and lush gardens that evolve as you progress.
- **Meaningful Narratives**: Faith-inspired stories and Jesus' parables to unlock at the end of each stage.
- **Offline Mode**: Play anytime, anywhere—no internet connection required.

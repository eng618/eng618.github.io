## Overview

**Tally Ho** is a modern, tactile scorekeeper and tally tracking app designed to eliminate friction on game night. Whether tracking scores for casual card games, complex board games, or multi-round tournaments, Tally Ho offers rapid score entry, saved player libraries, and round-by-round history logs — completely offline-first with zero account friction.

Experience Tally Ho across platforms:

## Key Features

- **Rapid Score Keypad**: Custom in-app numeric keypad designed specifically for quick score entry without clumsy native keyboard shifts.
- **Player Library & Roster Management**: Save frequent players and quickly spin up new matches without re-entering names every round.
- **Match & Round History**: Full round-by-round ledger to review past turns, point breakdowns, and game momentum.
- **Game Night Champion**: Dynamic winner celebration screens and champion announcements at match conclusion.
- **Offline First & Private**: All game state and player records remain stored locally on your device with no required sign-ups.
- **Multi-Theme & Tactile**: Thoughtfully designed dark and paper light themes with crisp audio effects and haptic feedback.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: React Native & Expo (Expo Router)
- **Language**: TypeScript
- **Styling**: NativeWind & Tailwind CSS
- **State Management**: Zustand / Local Storage
- **Platform**: Multi-platform distribution on Android (Google Play) and Web (Expo Web)

## How to use this list

1. Fill in the **Current stats** table first.
2. Work down the checklists by interval: **Every 5,000 miles / 6 months**, then **Every 15,000 miles**, etc.
3. When you complete something, mark it complete in your own system (Notion, phone notes, etc.) and update the relevant “last done” row above.

## ✅ Every 5,000 miles or 6 months

- [ ] Replace engine oil and oil filter (synthetic is fine).
- [ ] Rotate tires; check condition and tread depth.
- [ ] Inspect and adjust all fluid levels (coolant, brake, power steering if equipped, washer).
- [ ] Inspect brake pads, rotors, and brake lines for wear or leaks.
- [ ] Inspect wiper blades and replace if streaking.
- [ ] General walk‑around: lights, horn, underbody for leaks or damage.

> **If you off‑road or tow:**
>
> - [ ] Inspect ball joints, drive shaft boots, steering linkage.
> - [ ] Tighten chassis/body nuts and bolts.

---

## 🧰 Every 15,000 miles (in addition to 5k items)

- [ ] Re‑inspect suspension ball joints and dust covers.
- [ ] Inspect drive shaft boots, propeller shaft, and steering linkage/boots.
- [ ] Inspect exhaust system, heat shields, and hangers.
- [ ] Inspect engine air filter and cabin air filter; replace if dirty.
- [ ] Inspect cooling system, radiator, and condenser for leaks/blockage.

---

## 🔩 Every 30,000 miles

- [ ] Replace engine air filter (even if it looked “ok” earlier).
- [ ] Replace cabin air filter.
- [ ] Replace engine oil and filter (still every 5k overall).
- [ ] Rotate tires and inspect suspension: ball joints, bushings, steering gear/linkage, shocks/struts.
- [ ] Inspect brake lines and hoses; check parking brake operation.
- [ ] Inspect engine coolant level/condition, radiator, and hoses.
- [ ] Inspect rear differential oil and transfer case oil (4WD).
- [ ] Inspect transmission for leaks and fluid condition.
- [ ] Inspect fuel lines and tank area for leaks.

---

## 🔧 60,000–90,000 miles

- [ ] Continue all 5k and 15k checks.
- [ ] Replace spark plugs (many owners do at 60–90k; Toyota often targets 120k for iridium plugs).
- [ ] Inspect serpentine belt for cracks or glazing; replace if worn.
- [ ] Consider changing differential and transfer case fluids if you tow or off‑road frequently.

---

## ⏱ 100,000–120,000 miles and time-based items

- [ ] Replace spark plugs if not done earlier.
- [ ] Replace coolant (Toyota long‑life coolant is often first scheduled around 100k, then every ~50k).
- [ ] Brake fluid: flush about every 3 years regardless of miles.
- [ ] Transmission fluid: many owners choose a drain & fill in the 60–100k range for long‑term reliability.
- [ ] Accessory/serpentine belt: replace if not already done by ~100k or 7–10 years.

---

## ⚙️ Alignment notes

- [ ] Have alignment checked when you notice pulling, a crooked steering wheel, or uneven tire wear.
- [ ] Check alignment after impacts (potholes, curbs) or any suspension changes.
- [ ] Many shops suggest checking alignment about every 15–30k miles or when you replace tires.

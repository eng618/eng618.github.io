Raleigh bermudagrass cheat sheet (home lawn). Based primarily on NC State guidance, with local timing cues.

Primary source: [NC State Bermudagrass Maintenance Calendar](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/bermudagrass-lawn-maintenance-calendar)

## 5 key rules

1. **Do not fertilize at first green-up**; wait until turf is mostly green (usually mid/late April).
2. **Fertilize during active growth only** (mid-April to early August), every 4–6 weeks.
3. **Use pre-emergent on schedule**: spring (late Feb/early Mar, plus split follow-up) and fall (early Sept).
4. **Mow low and often**: keep Bermuda at 1–2" with a rotary mower; follow the one-third rule.
5. **Avoid spraying stressed turf**: skip herbicides during heat/drought stress.

## Seasonal schedule (Raleigh)

### Jan–Feb (dormant)

- Fertilizer: none.
- Weed control: late Feb/early Mar spring pre-emergent before soil stays near 55°F.
- Mowing: usually none.

### Mar–Apr (green-up)

- Fertilizer: wait until mostly green, then apply ~1 lb N per 1,000 sq ft.
- Weed control: broadleaf spot treatment on mild days once Bermuda is waking up.
- Mowing: cleanup cut at green-up, then maintain 1–2".

### May–Aug (peak growth)

- Fertilizer: ~1 lb N per 1,000 sq ft every 4–6 weeks (roughly May, Jun, Jul, early Aug).
- Weed control: spot-treat breakthroughs; crabgrass may need repeat apps 7–10 days apart.
- Mowing: every 3–7 days depending on growth; stay in the 1–2" range.

### Sep–Oct (slowdown)

- Fertilizer: stop regular nitrogen after early Aug (or by early Sept at latest).
- Weed control: apply fall pre-emergent in early Sept; resume winter broadleaf control as needed.
- Mowing: continue until growth stops (often late Oct/Nov).

### Nov–Dec (dormant)

- Fertilizer: none.
- Weed control: optional cool-season broadleaf spot treatments on mild days.
- Mowing: generally none unless overseeded.

## Quick timing table

| Task                    | Typical Raleigh window                                 |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Spring pre-emergent     | Late Feb–early Mar; split follow-up late Apr–early May |
| Fall pre-emergent       | Early September                                        |
| Nitrogen fertilizer     | Mid/late Apr, May, Jun, Jul, early Aug                 |
| Broadleaf post-emergent | As needed Mar–Oct (mild temps, non-stressed turf)      |
| Crabgrass post-emergent | Late spring/summer; repeat 2–3 times if needed         |
| Start mowing            | Late Mar/early Apr at green-up                         |
| Stop mowing             | Late Oct–Nov when growth stops                         |
| Rotary mowing height    | 1–2" during growing season                             |

## Practical targets

- **Seasonal nitrogen total**: commonly ~3–5 lb N per 1,000 sq ft per year.
- **Rotary mower setup**: 1–1.5" in spring, 1–2" in summer/early fall.
- **Reel mower only**: lower heights (about 0.5–1") require a level yard and very frequent mowing.

## Notes

- Calibrate spreader/sprayer and follow product labels.
- Use soil test results to fine-tune fertilizer and potassium decisions.

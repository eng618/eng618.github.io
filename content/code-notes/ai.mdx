## Basic Understanding of AI Prompting

Prompt engineering is the art of communicating with a generative AI model. Here are some key principles:

<Accordion type="single" collapsible className="mt-4 mb-8 w-full">
  <AccordionItem value="item-1">
    <AccordionTrigger>Be Specific and Clear</AccordionTrigger>
    <AccordionContent>
      Instead of asking "Write about dogs," try "Write a 300-word blog post about the benefits of adopting a senior dog,
      targeting first-time pet owners."
    </AccordionContent>
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="item-2">
    <AccordionTrigger>Provide Context</AccordionTrigger>
    <AccordionContent>
      Give the AI background information. "Act as a senior software engineer reviewing my code. Here is the context..."
    </AccordionContent>
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem value="item-3">
    <AccordionTrigger>Use Examples (Few-Shot Prompting)</AccordionTrigger>
    <AccordionContent>
      Show the AI what you want. Provide a few examples of the desired input and output format before asking it to
      generate the final response.
    </AccordionContent>
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
